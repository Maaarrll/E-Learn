ALLEARN Teacher Card

Keep this next to the classroom laptop.

Before class

  1. Plug in the laptop and turn on the Wi-Fi router.
  2. Connect the laptop to the classroom Wi-Fi.
  3. Double-click Start ALLEARN on the desktop. Keep the black window open.
  4. Show the page with the QR code on the projector.
  5. Click Open the Admin page, log in, and switch the class to Open.

Students

  1. Connect to the classroom Wi-Fi.
  2. Turn off mobile data.
  3. Scan the QR code (or type the address shown under it).
  4. First time: Sign up with their LRN. After that: log in with their username or full name.

After class

  1. Switch the class to Closed in the Admin page.
  2. Close the black ALLEARN window.
  3. Every Friday: copy the classroom-data folder to the USB drive.

If something goes wrong

  • One phone can't open it: check it's on the classroom Wi-Fi and mobile data is off. Type the address instead of scanning.
  • No phone can open it: close the black window and double-click Start ALLEARN again.
  • "The class is closed": open the class in the Admin page.
  • A student forgot their password: they tap "Forgot password" and enter their name and LRN.
  • The black window shows an error: take a photo of it and send it to support.
